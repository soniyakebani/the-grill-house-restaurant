A kitchen run by one family.

The Grill House opened in 1998 with one small kitchen and a short menu that hasn't changed much since. We still make pasta by hand every morning, buy vegetables from three nearby farms, and keep the dining room small enough that the kitchen knows most tables by name.

No shortcuts, no reheating — everything on your table was cooked that day.
